# Citizen reports: routing and replies

**Team.** T02, the reports team. **Data.** Restricted (phone numbers, locations, photos).

Citizens report potholes, broken lights and blocked pavements through the app and the call centre: 90,000 reports a year, growing 15% a year. Each report carries text, a location, a phone number and sometimes a photo. The team wants each report routed to the right department and a reply drafted for the citizen.

**Where to start.** Day 2's labelled reports (Lab 2.1) and Day 3's usage logs of T02, including its breach in May and June.
