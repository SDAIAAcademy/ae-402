Pass: 70% overall, and at least 60% on this rubric and on the supervised checks. The repository is 60% of the result, the defence 20%, the supervised checks 20%.

Full marks on the platform slice means it runs: the gateway refuses Restricted data to an external model in a test, its log keeps no prompt text, and the showback reconciles within 2%. Fitness checks score only if they run in the repository's pipeline and fail on a planted bad design. One review criterion checks that responsible-AI controls (oversight and evaluation targets) are designed in from the start.
